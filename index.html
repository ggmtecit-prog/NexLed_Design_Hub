Introduction

This document defines the visual and graphic system of Nexled, establishing guidelines for color, typography, composition, and graphic style to ensure consistency across every application. It also explains the strategic thinking behind the system and the decisions that shaped it.

Nexled was created within TEC IT to enter the LED lighting sector with a clear focus on technical quality and long-term performance. Rather than competing on price, the brand focuses on delivering high-quality, durable, and repairable LED solutions — building trust through engineering, precision, and responsible design. Its scale enables flexibility, allowing Nexled to adapt products and create tailored solutions for each client.

As the brand prepares to expand from Portugal into Europe and beyond, a stronger and more coherent identity became essential. This rebranding initiative was proposed and developed by Gonçalo Mendonça, addressing missing fundamentals such as a defined color palette, mission, values, and a cohesive visual system. Some elements, like the logo, were retained due to legal constraints, but the result is a complete and functional identity built within those limits.

A brand is a living system. This Brand Book is a working document, supported by digital platforms to allow updates as the company and its context evolve.
